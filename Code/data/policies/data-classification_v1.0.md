# Data Classification Policy v1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Supersedes:** None
**Superseded By:** v2.0 (effective September 1, 2025)

## 1. Purpose
To establish a framework for classifying company data based on its sensitivity and value to ensure appropriate protection measures are applied.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all data created, collected, processed, stored, or transmitted by the company, regardless of format or location.

## 3. Data Classification Levels

### 3.1 Public Data
- Definition: Information that can be freely shared with the public without harm
- Examples: Press releases, marketing materials, published financial reports
- Protection: No specific controls required beyond basic integrity
- Handling: Can be shared without restriction

### 3.2 Internal Data
- Definition: Information intended for company use only
- Examples: Internal memos, procedural documents, organizational charts
- Protection: Basic access controls, no encryption required for storage
- Handling: Share within company only, no external distribution

### 3.3 Confidential Data
- Definition: Information that could cause harm if disclosed unauthorized
- Examples: Employee records, customer lists, financial projections
- Protection: Access controls, encryption required for transmission
- Handling: Need-to-know basis, confidentiality agreements for third parties

### 3.4 Restricted Data
- Definition: Highly sensitive information requiring maximum protection
- Examples: Trade secrets, encryption keys, merger/acquisition details
- Protection: Strong access controls, encryption at rest and in transit, DLP
- Handling: Limited to specific individuals, strict usage monitoring

## 4. Classification Responsibilities
- **Data Owner**: Responsible for classifying data under their authority
- **Data Custodian**: Responsible for implementing security controls
- **All Employees**: Must classify data they create or handle according to guidelines
- **Data Stewards**: Assist with classification in large-scale implementations

## 5. Data Handling Requirements

### 5.1 Storage
- Public: No special requirements
- Internal: Company-approved systems
- Confidential: Access-controlled systems with logging
- Restricted: Encrypted storage, access logging, limited retention

### 5.2 Transmission
- Public: No encryption required
- Internal: Company network acceptable
- Confidential: Encryption required for external transmission
- Restricted: Encryption required for all transmission, approved channels only

### 5.3 Destruction
- All data must be destroyed according to retention schedules
- Confidential and Restricted data: Secure deletion methods required
- Certification of destruction for sensitive media

## 6. Exceptions
- Legal holds may override classification requirements
- Regulatory requirements may dictate specific handling
- Exceptions must be documented and approved by Information Security
