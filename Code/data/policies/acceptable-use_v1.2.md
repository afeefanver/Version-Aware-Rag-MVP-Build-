# Acceptable Use Policy v1.2
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025
**Supersedes:** v1.1
**Superseded By:** v2.0 (effective December 1, 2025)

## 1. Purpose
To define appropriate use of company technology resources and protect those resources from misuse, damage, or unauthorized access.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporaries, and other workers at the company, including all personnel affiliated with third parties using company technology resources.

## 3. General Use Principles
Company technology resources are provided for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted when it does not interfere with job performance, system operations, or other users.

## 4. permitted Activities
- Accessing information and resources necessary for job performance
- Communication with colleagues, clients, and partners for business purposes
- Use of approved software and services for business tasks
- Limited personal use during breaks (email, web browsing, etc.)
- Training and professional development activities

## 5. Prohibited Activities

### 5.1 Illegal Activities
- Any activity that violates local, state, federal, or international law
- Unauthorized access to computer systems (hacking)
- Distribution of viruses, malware, or other harmful code
- Copyright infringement or software piracy
- Possession or distribution of illegal materials

### 5.2 Security Violations
- Sharing passwords or authentication credentials
- Attempting to bypass security controls or monitoring
- Connecting unauthorized devices to the network
- Disabling or tampering with security software
- Attempting to access unauthorized systems or data

### 5.3 Inappropriate Content
- Creating, accessing, or distributing obscene, pornographic, or sexually explicit material
- Creating, accessing, or distributing harassing, hateful, or discriminatory content
- Creating, accessing, or distributing defamatory or threatening content
- Engaging in cyberbullying or online harassment

### 5.4 Resource Misuse
- Excessive personal use that interferes with job responsibilities
- Running personal business activities using company resources
- Unauthorized use of company resources for political campaigns
- Streaming media or downloading large files that consume excessive bandwidth
- Cryptocurrency mining or other resource-intensive unauthorized activities

### 5.5 Data Handling Violations
- Unauthorized copying, sharing, or removal of company data
- Sending confidential information via unsecured channels
- Using company resources to process personal data without authorization
- Violating data privacy policies or confidentiality agreements

## 6. Electronic Communications
- Company email systems are for business communication
- Professional and respectful tone required in all communications
- No expectation of privacy in company systems
- Monitoring may occur for security, maintenance, or policy compliance
- Automatic forwarding of company email to personal accounts prohibited

## 7. Internet Usage
- Web browsing for business purposes permitted
- Access to job-related websites and resources encouraged
- Personal internet use permitted during designated breaks
- Web filtering may be employed to block inappropriate or malicious sites
- Downloading software requires IT approval and security scanning

## 8. Mobile and Remote Access
- Company-issued mobile devices subject to same usage policies
- Personal devices used for company work must comply with BYOD policy
- Remote access must use approved secure methods (VPN, secure desktop)
- Company data on personal devices must be protected as if on company premises
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately to IT security

## 9. Social Media
- Personal social media use during work hours limited to breaks
- Company-affiliated social media accounts require authorization
- Employees must not represent themselves as speaking for the company without authorization
- Confidential company information must not be disclosed on social media
- Employees should be mindful of how personal posts reflect on their professional reputation

## 10. Enforcement
- Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Illegal activities will be reported to law enforcement
- Company reserves right to examine any data stored on company systems
- Policy violations may result in loss of technology privileges

## 11. Revision History

- v1.1: Added social media and remote work sections (June 1, 2025)
- v1.2: Clarified prohibited activities and enforcement (February 1, 2025)
