## 1. Introduction
This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website, mobile applications, and online services ("Services"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

## 2. Information We Collect
### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- Account Information: Email address, password (hashed), phone number, profile information
- Payment Information: Credit card details (tokenized by payment processor), billing address
- Communications: Messages sent through our support system, feedback, survey responses
- Documentation: Identification documents uploaded for verification purposes (when required)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution
- Connection Information: IP address, ISP, connection type
- Usage Metrics: Pages viewed, features used, actions taken, referral source, session duration
- Location Information: 
  * Coarse location: Derived from IP address (city-level)
  * Fine location: GPS data from mobile devices (only with explicit consent)
- Tracking Technologies: 
  * Cookies: HTTP cookies, HTML5 local storage, session storage
  * Pixel tags: For email open tracking and conversion measurement
  * Fingerprinting techniques: For fraud prevention (limited use with disclosure)

### 2.3 Information from Other Sources
- Social Media: Profile information when you connect social media accounts (based on your privacy settings)
- Business Partners: Jointly offered products or services
- Public Records: Information from publicly available sources for identity verification and fraud prevention
- Data Brokers: Demographic and interest data (only for advertising purposes with opt-in consent)

## 3. Legal Bases for Processing
We process your personal information based on one or more of the following legal grounds:
- **Consent**: When you have given clear consent for specific processing activities
- **Contract Necessity**: When processing is necessary to fulfill our contractual obligations to you
- **Legal Obligation**: When processing is required to comply with applicable laws and regulations
- **Legitimate Interests**: When processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms
- **Vital Interests**: When processing is necessary to protect someone's life
- **Public Task**: When processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

### 6.1 Retention Periods
- Account Information: Until account deletion plus 90 days for backup recovery
- Transaction and Financial Information: Minimum 7 years for tax and regulatory compliance
- Marketing Communications: Until you opt-out or withdraw consent
- Usage and Analytics Data: 24 months in identifiable form, then aggregated indefinitely
- Support Communications: 3 years after ticket resolution
- Legal Holds: Indefinitely when subject to legal preservation obligations

### 6.2 Deletion Processes
When you request deletion of your account or personal information:
- We initiate deletion within 30 days of receiving a verifiable request
- We delete data from active systems, backups, and archives where technically feasible
- We retain only what is necessary for legal compliance, dispute resolution, or safety/security
- We provide confirmation of deletion completion upon request

## 7. Data Security Program
We maintain a comprehensive information security program designed to protect your personal information against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access.

### 7.1 Technical Controls
- Encryption: AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- Key Management: Hardware security modules for cryptographic key management
- Network Security: Firewalls, intrusion detection/prevention systems, web application firewalls
- Endpoint Security: Anti-malware, disk encryption, mobile device management
- Application Security: Secure coding practices, regular penetration testing, vulnerability scanning
- Monitoring: Security information and event management (SIEM), user behavior analytics

### 7.2 Organizational Measures
- Access Controls: Principle of least privilege, role-based access control, just-in-time access
- Identity Management: Multi-factor authentication for all privileged access, single sign-on
- Training: Mandatory annual security and privacy awareness training for all employees
- Policies: Comprehensive information security, data protection, and acceptable use policies
- Governance: Dedicated security team, incident response plan, business continuity planning
- Assessments: Regular risk assessments, third-party audits, compliance evaluations

### 7.3 Third-Party Risk Management
- Vendor Security Assessments: Mandatory for all third parties handling personal information
- Contractual Requirements: Security and data protection clauses in all vendor agreements
- Continuous Monitoring: Ongoing assessment of third-party security practices
- Incident Response: Coordinated response plans for security incidents involving third parties

## 8. Your Privacy Rights
You may have the following rights depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, etc.):

### 8.1 Access and Portability
- Right to Access: Obtain confirmation of whether we process your personal information and access that information
- Right to Data Portability: Receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format

### 8.2 Rectification and Restriction
- Right to Rectification: Have inaccurate or incomplete personal information corrected
- Right to Restriction: Obtain limitation of processing of your personal information

### 8.3 Erasure and Objection
- Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Have your personal information deleted under certain circumstances
- Right to Object: Object to processing of your personal information for direct marketing or legitimate interests

### 8.4 Automated Decision Making and Profiling
- Right to Not Be Subject: To decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects
- Right to Explanation: Obtain meaningful information about the logic involved in automated decision-making

### 8.5 Withdrawal of Consent
- Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing at any time where processing is based on consent

### 8.6 Exercise of Rights
To exercise your rights, please contact us using the information provided below. We will respond to your request within one month, subject to extension for complex requests.
